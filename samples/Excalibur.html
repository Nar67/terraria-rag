The Excalibur attacking Target Dummies. Note how it only hits three of the four Target Dummies.

The Excalibur is a Hardmode, post-one mechanical boss broadsword. When swung, a large yellow aura follows the swing, reaching about twice the distance of the sword itself. The aura can hit up to three enemies.

Its best modifier is Legendary.

Notes[edit]

  • The Excalibur will always knock enemies away from the player, regardless of the direction it is swung.
  • The Excalibur's aura is fully bright in the dark and releases light-emitting particles.

Tips[edit]

  • The Excalibur can be swung while walking backwards, which is useful for knocking back enemies during retreat.

Trivia[edit]

  • Prior to the 1.2 update, the Excalibur was the strongest sword in the game.
  • Excalibur is the mythical sword of King Arthur from Arthurian legends. While some sources conflate Excalibur with the "Sword In The Stone" by which Arthur proved his kingship, more reliable sources have them as two separate swords.
    • In Terraria, the Sword In The Stone is separately represented by the Enchanted Sword item.
  • Excalibur is later given to Arthur by the Lady In The Lake, which is also referenced in Terraria.
  • The Enchanted Sword enemy shares the Excalibur's sprite design.
  • The Excalibur appears in the video game Palworld as a part of a crossover.
